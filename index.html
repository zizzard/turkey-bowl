How the numbers work

Baseline
Season PPR points per game, blended with one game at a modest positional average so one- or two-game samples don't run wild.
Player props
Where sportsbooks post player lines, the projection comes from them: passing yards × 0.04, passing TDs × 4 (from the TD over/under odds), rushing and receiving yards × 0.1, receptions × 1, and expected TDs × 6 from anytime-TD odds (after removing the book's cut). Prop lines are medians and fantasy scoring skews high, so these get a 10% bump to put them on the same scale as season averages. Most numbers come from First Down Studio’s prop-based stat lines (yards, receptions, expected TDs), rescored for full PPR; a few depth players use lines pulled from betting previews. Everyone else uses the model below. Props as of Nov 25.
Vegas adjustment
Implied team total = (game total ± spread) ÷ 2. Projection is scaled by implied points ÷ 23 (league average), damped to 75%. D/STs scale the other way, off the opponent's implied total. Kicker and D/ST projections are then pulled halfway to their position average, because their week-to-week scoring is mostly noise. Lines as of Nov 25.
VBD
Projection minus the replacement player: the Nth best still available, where N is the open starting spots left at that position across the league. It recalculates after every pick.
Pick advice
For the team on the clock, the board simulates everyone else drafting until your next turn and shows how many points each position drops if you wait. The biggest drop is the pick.
Draft position
2,000 mock drafts where every team picks by live VBD with some randomness, scored by total projected starting points.
